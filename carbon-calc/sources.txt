1. MASTER REPORTING FRAMEWORK (GREENHOUSE GAS PROTOCOL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Standard: GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard
- Lead Organizations: World Resources Institute (WRI) & WBCSD
- Official Link: https://ghgprotocol.org/calculation-tools-and-guidance
- What We Took:
  * The division into Scope 1 (direct engine fuel combustion) and Scope 2
    (indirect emissions from charging EVs via the electricity grid).
  * The Fuel-Based Activity Method:
    Fuel Consumed = Distance / Fuel Efficiency
    CO2 Emitted   = Fuel Consumed * Emission Factor


2. FUEL EMISSION FACTORS (SCOPE 1 DIRECT COMBUSTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. PETROL (2.31 kg CO2 / Liter)
   - Primary Source: UK Government DESNZ / DEFRA Conversion Factors
     Link: https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/government-conversion-factors-for-company-reporting
   - Cross-Verified With: US EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub
     Link: https://www.epa.gov/climateleadership/ghg-emission-factors-hub
   - Note on Multi-Source: Both UK DEFRA and US EPA publish standard mobile 
     combustion tables that calculate to ~2.31 kg CO2 per liter of finished motor petrol.

B. DIESEL (2.68 kg CO2 / Liter)
   - Primary Source: UK Government DESNZ / DEFRA Conversion Factors
     Link: https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/government-conversion-factors-for-company-reporting
   - Cross-Verified With: US EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub & GHG Protocol Transport Tool
     Link: https://www.epa.gov/climateleadership/ghg-emission-factors-hub
   - Note on Multi-Source: Standard commercial pure mineral diesel is universally 
     benchmarked across DEFRA, US EPA, and GHG Protocol between 2.68 and 2.69 kg CO2/L.

C. CNG / COMPRESSED NATURAL GAS (2.75 kg CO2 / kg)
   - Primary Source: IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) EFDB
     Link: https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/EFDB/main.php
   - Cross-Verified With: UK DEFRA Fuel Tables & GHG Protocol Gaseous Fuels
     Link: https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/government-conversion-factors-for-company-reporting
   - Note on Multi-Source: Because CNG is measured by mass (kg) rather than volume (liters),
     the factor originates from the IPCC Mobile Road Transport Chapter and is mirrored
     in DEFRA's gaseous fuels database.


3. ELECTRIC VEHICLE GRID INTENSITY (SCOPE 2 INDIRECT EMISSIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Value: 0.475 kg CO2 / kWh
- Primary Source: International Energy Agency (IEA) Emissions Factors Database
  Link: https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-product/emissions-factors-2023
- Cross-Verified With: World Bank & GHG Protocol Global Electricity Baselines
- Note on Multi-Source: Electric cars emit zero tailpipe CO2, so this factor 
  accounts for the carbon released by power plants generating grid electricity to charge the EV battery.


4. SPEED-CONGESTION EFFICIENCY CURVE (ENGINE THERMAL LOSS & DRAG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Source: US Department of Energy (DOE) & Oak Ridge National Laboratory
- Official Link: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/driveHabits.jsp
- What We Took:
  * Speed <= 25 km/h : 30% fuel efficiency penalty (Heavy stop-and-go traffic / idling)
  * Speed 26-50 km/h : 15% fuel efficiency penalty (Moderate urban traffic / repeated acceleration)
  * Speed 51-85 km/h : 0% penalty (Optimal steady-state engine cruising efficiency)
  * Speed > 85 km/h  : 10% penalty (Highway aerodynamic drag loss where drag increases with speed squared)
